Luis Bauer

M.Sc. Physics student at the University of Tübingen · Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems

Tübingen, Germany

Hi, I’m Luis. I am a master’s student in physics (M.Sc.) at the University of Tübingen. For my master’s thesis I work on predicting solar storms using machine learning at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in Tübingen, supervised by Bernhard Schölkopf.

My interests include generative AI, computer vision and machine learning for physics. For my bachelor’s thesis, also at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems, I worked on flow matching and implemented Conditional Flow Matching on MNIST in PyTorch: a neural network learns a time-dependent velocity field, and new images are generated by integrating the corresponding ODE from Gaussian noise to the data distribution.

You can find more on the projects, repositories and CV pages, or on GitHub.
